Cell City

Welcome to Cell City!!

Cell City is an interactive educational website that represents a biological cell as a small, functioning city. In this concept, each organelle is given a city-based role—for example, the nucleus acts like the city’s central administration, mitochondria like power plants, ribosomes like protein factories, and the Golgi apparatus like a packaging and distribution center. Users can explore these structures, understand their real biological functions, and follow processes such as DNA → RNA → protein and nutrient → ATP production. The project also includes a Bioinformatics Lab with features such as DNA sequence analysis, GC-content calculation, transcription, translation, codon exploration, and mutation visualization, making Cell City both a creative learning environment and a practical demonstration of web development and bioinformatics concepts.

Explore the Cell

In Cell City, the different organelles will be represented as important parts of a functioning city, making their biological roles easier to understand through familiar real-world examples. The nucleus will act as the city hall, mitochondria as power plants, ribosomes as protein factories, the rough and smooth ER as manufacturing and processing networks, the Golgi apparatus as a packaging and distribution center, lysosomes as recycling and waste-management centers, vesicles as delivery vehicles, and the cytoskeleton as the city's internal road system. The cell membrane will form the city's outer boundary, while the cytoplasm will represent the environment in which everything operates. This city-based representation will allow users to visually explore how different organelles work individually and how they cooperate to keep the cell functioning.
